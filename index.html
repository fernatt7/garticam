QUICK FIELD GUIDE

How to play

  • Draw: point with your index finger. Keep it extended briefly to begin a line.
  • Pause a stroke: pinch your thumb and index finger together. Release to continue with a fresh segment.
  • Pause drawing: make the phone-call hand sign to toggle drawing mode off or on.
  • Use the toolbar: hover your fingertip over a tool for a moment, or use a mouse or touchscreen.
  • Canvas only: switch between the drawing board alone and the camera with the drawing overlaid.
  • Landmarks: turn the hand skeleton overlay on or off for tracking checks.
  • Multiplayer: the drawer sees a secret word; the guesser submits guesses in chat. Take turns before the timer ends.
